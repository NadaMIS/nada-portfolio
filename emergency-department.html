01 / Operational question

Predict demand before demand becomes a queue.

The project analyzed 2022 emergency-department data from the Royal Commission Hospital in Jubail. The operational problems were practical: fluctuating patient volumes, waiting times, and the difficulty of anticipating severity.

The goal was to understand visit patterns, forecast patient volume, classify severity and identify groups of visits with distinct waiting-time and length-of-stay behavior.

02 / The rhythm of the department

The day has a pulse.

Arrivals were lowest around 3–6 AM, rose through the morning, and peaked between roughly 10 AM and 4 PM. That pattern translates directly into a staffing and capacity-planning signal.

Across the cleaned dataset, average waiting time was about 33.3 minutes. Daily arrivals also fluctuated materially across the year.

04 / Forecasting result

SARIMA won this round.

The value of the forecast was operational rather than decorative: expected demand can inform scheduling, staffing and resource allocation before high-volume periods arrive.

05 / Severity classification

Accuracy wasn’t the whole story.

75%

Decision Tree overall accuracy

The Decision Tree reached about 75% overall accuracy, while the balanced Random Forest + SMOTE model was around 73%. But the dataset was highly imbalanced, so overall accuracy alone could hide weak performance on rare severity levels.

The Random Forest improved minority-class recall and F1 in the report, making the evaluation a useful lesson in choosing metrics that match the operational risk—not simply choosing the largest accuracy number.

07 / Decision signal

What the hospital can act on.

Staff around the arrival curve. Treat severity prediction as a risk problem. Use patient-flow segments to plan beds and clinical capacity—not just front-desk queues.
08 / Data preparation

96,537 → 90,036.

After handling missing values, the project retained 90,036 complete records. Arrival and discharge timestamps were engineered into usable time features; gender and severity were encoded; and waiting time and length of stay were winsorized at the 1st and 99th percentiles to reduce the influence of extreme observations.
