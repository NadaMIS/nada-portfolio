Case study /03 Business optimization Team of 4 · real client Excel Solver · linear programming

A café losing customers. Where should the money go?

Mostakan is a tea room in Al-Hofuf. Orders fell sharply between early 2024 and early 2025. Working with the owner, we turned a fixed marketing budget into an optimization problem across five social platforms.

SAR 5,000monthly marketing budget
5platforms: TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat, Instagram, X
≈1,202people reached a month at the optimum
0.23more people reached per extra riyal
Problem

Most of the café's 20 products lost profit year over year while operating costs stayed flat, so the problem was fewer customers, not higher costs. The owner planned to bring pastry in-house, which helps margins but doesn't bring people through the door.

max Z = 0.293·TikTok + 0.207·Snapchat + 0.138·Instagram + 0.129·X + 0.233·YouTube
s.t. total = 5,000 · each ≥ 300 · each ≤ cost of reaching all its local users
Findings

Two platforms take 82% of the budget.

TikTok has the best reach per riyal but a small local audience, so it hits its cap at SAR 1,749. YouTube takes the rest. The other three stay at the SAR 300 minimum.

Optimal monthly allocation · SAR

Extra money pays off only up to a point.

Each extra riyal reaches 0.23 more people until the budget hits about SAR 6,134, when YouTube's cap binds too. After that, money flows to weaker platforms. Above SAR 7,823 there is no local audience left to buy.

Projected monthly reach vs total budget

The "be everywhere" rule costs reach.

Forcing SAR 300 onto every platform has a price. The shadow prices show how many people each forced riyal loses compared with spending it on YouTube.

People lost per riyal forced to the SAR 300 minimum
Recommendation
  1. Run it for three monthsApply the allocation and track reach and new customers per platform.
  2. Grow the budget moderatelyUp to about SAR 1,134 more keeps the full 0.23 return per riyal.
  3. Re-optimize with real dataReplace estimated coefficients with the café's own campaign results.
My role

A joint project: the four of us did every stage together, from owner interviews to sales analysis, the model and the sensitivity analysis. Team: Nusaybah Aljaafari, Saud Alshammari, Taghreed Al Qahtani.

Limits

Coefficients come from estimated local user counts and published ad rates, which change by season. The model maximizes reach, not profit.
